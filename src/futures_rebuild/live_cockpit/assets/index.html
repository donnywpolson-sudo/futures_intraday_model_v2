How should small history gaps be handled?

The cockpit cannot collect historical bars while it is closed. It can repair small gaps when you return.

  • Automatic repairs run at most once every 24 hours.
  • The provider estimate must be $0.05 or less.
  • Larger, unknown, or failed updates always stop for review.
  • The estimate is not guaranteed final billing.
