## Experiment and clock

ES, NQ, CL and GC are separate one-standard-contract experiments. The only
selection dates are the 1,305 independent weekdays in 2018-2022, including
holidays and dates with no observations. UTC event timestamps use the
`America/Los_Angeles` clock for decisions: 06:30:05 through 11:59:05 every
minute. Entry is the following minute boundary; scheduled exit is 60 minutes
later. The last entry is 12:00 and last scheduled exit 13:00. Earlier completed
bars may provide history. An instruction to exit does not guarantee liquidation.

The preparation stores every exact inner and outer date range before fitting.
Eight consecutive outer audits contain 63 weekdays each; each has four inner
63-date audits. The existing date purge and one-date embargo are preserved,
with training labels strictly mature before the first scheduled audit decision.
Observed training-date minima are 252 inner and 505 outer/final. The model
refreshes only between these approximately quarterly outer periods, never daily.
Fixed-window results do not establish all-session performance.

## Inputs, assumptions and missingness

Only owned, permitted OHLCV-1m and applicable instrument/status metadata enter
this experiment. OHLCV-1s is deferred timing/path evidence, with no missing-price
fallback. Hour/day bars and statistics are excluded. L1 remains validation-only.

The source adapter validates canonical numeric types, OHLCV consistency, tick
alignment, ordering, source identity and unit consistency. It does not relabel
the strict calendar/source certificate as passing. Historical use still needs
the existing trusted immutable-release admission route and the certified
research gateway. A matching hash detects drift; it does not establish source
validity, publication timing or research permission.

Bar end plus five seconds is an explicit replay assumption, with later declared
eligibility respected. Historical revisions and actual application delivery
remain unresolved. Instrument definitions must be available at the decision.
Contract changes reset the 61-bar history. Features and targets never cross
actual contracts; roll/mapping timing remains conditional on the supplied
historical contract sequence unless independently verified.

The clock never uses future outcome availability or retrospective closing
times. Causally known closures/halts may suppress entry. Missing history causes
abstention; separately evidenced no-trade intervals and outages are reported as
coverage diagnostics. Missing bars alone remain unresolved coverage and never
prove closure. Forecasts with missing targets remain in the complete ledger.

## Models, policy and selection

The five existing baseline features are 20-minute tick change, 20-minute tick
volatility, close location in the 20-minute range, volume relative to the prior
20-bar median, and price-direction-weighted volume. The second pack adds the
existing 60-minute tick change and volatility. The volume feature is an OHLCV
proxy, not measured aggressor order flow. Quartiles and interactions are absent.

Each pack crosses Ridge penalties 0.1/1/10 and XGBoost depths 2/3: ten candidates
per market and 40 new configuration identities. Existing date-balanced weights,
training-only constant/duplicate removal, Ridge standardization and normalized
regularization are retained. XGBoost uses 100 deterministic CPU rounds, seed
62817, eta .05, max_bin 64, lambda .01, min_child_weight .05 and a minimum of
20 observed training dates per leaf. Other exact settings are in the preparation.
The endpoint opening-print target is expressed in ticks and is not a fill.

Every eligible forecast is generated before replay attaches economic outcomes.
One pending/open position occupies the market; intervening forecasts are saved
but cannot pyramid, reverse or alter the scheduled exit. Exits precede later
decisions. Predictions strictly exceeding round-trip cost select long; those
below its negative select short; equality abstains. There are no stops, profit
targets or signal-based early exits.

The shared replay checks calendar permission only for a flat market; pending and
open positions retain their original exit instructions. ALLOWED requires a
supported open interval covering the decision, entry and full holding period.
An exit exactly at a close is blocked. BLOCKED and UNKNOWN abstain without
removing forecasts, labels or zero-trade dates. Conflicting schedules are UNKNOWN.

Two views are frozen for the timing diagnostic. `evidence_only` uses an archive
capture with a matching content digest only after its recorded timestamp.
`assumed_availability` assumes the applicable daily schedule was available by
00:00 Pacific that date. Neither view supplies missing schedule content or
ordinary-session coverage. Unrepresented dates remain UNKNOWN. Retrospective
halt evidence explains gaps but cannot suppress earlier decisions.

The timing-only path uses the same position state machine with no predictions,
costs or profitability calculations. It starts flat for each of the 19 distinct
frozen windows (40 original inner/outer slots), never resets daily, and stops a
window at its first unresolved execution. Later windows are independent probes,
not a continuation of a failed market campaign. Long and short timing are checked
for equality; no-trade results are explicitly identified as no orders attempted.
The source/opportunity ledger is saved once per market. Gap reconciliation uses
market and decision timestamp, with separate unique-decision, distinct-window and
original-slot counts. Benchmark completion does not certify model-policy prices
or fills. Existing fee, source and research admission gates still apply.

Round-trip tick allowances are ES/NQ 3/6/12 and CL/GC 5/10/20 for
base/adverse/severe, plus $10 per round trip. These are assumptions, not
historical estimates or upper bounds. Applicable independent standard-contract
fee evidence is unresolved; a substitution changes the frozen design.

Inner selection maximizes mean daily adverse net dollars over the same complete
audit dates, including zero-trade dates. No trade scores zero and wins a zero
tie. Exact model ties prefer Ridge, fewer declared features, stronger Ridge
regularization or shallower trees, then stable identifier. Only declared leaf
support failure invalidates an individual candidate. An integrity failure or
unresolved scheduled execution stops that market and preserves partial evidence.

An entry without an exact same-contract reference is unresolved execution, not
a retrospective no trade. Missing exit evidence retains the position, preceding
ledger and failure reason. Complete net P&L, mean and drawdown become unavailable;
resolved-prefix amounts are explicitly partial. No substituted price, dropped
trade, candidate replacement or favorable date restriction is allowed.

No trade, the date-balanced training-mean predictor, always-long-when-flat and
always-short-when-flat use the same clock, eligibility, occupancy and costs.
They are diagnostics, never outer-result selection alternatives. Base and severe
scenarios replay whole policies with their respective thresholds. Repricing the
fixed adverse trade list is reported separately.

Reports retain daily/fold P&L, gross/net/cost dollars, trades, trading and empty
dates, unresolved coverage, realized daily drawdown, long/short exposure,
largest winning/losing days, profit concentration and fixed-trade cost headroom.
Prediction errors compare the fitted model with zero change and the
date-balanced training mean on common observed targets. Missing-target dates are
disclosed, without imputing zero errors. There is no capital return or portfolio
Sharpe claim; drawdown is closed-trade daily reference P&L, not account equity.

## Custody, final fitting and L1 challenge

Every inner candidate result remains saved. Models and forecasts are written
before their economic replay. The last outer period's inner winner supplies the
final model, refitted only on mature permitted development labels. When no trade
wins, its inactive policy and fitted candidate model are both saved. Feature
order, transformations, costs, units, dependency versions and cutoffs accompany
the model. Reload predictions must match exactly, without scoring the final fit.

The existing create-only canonical JSON writer and file lease protect task
outputs. Completed checkpoints verify the operation identity and all saved artifacts before reuse. Failed
or interrupted checkpoints remain sealed and cannot silently execute again.
Markets run sequentially. Source and predecessor evidence remain untouched.
Generated campaigns have separate declarations and never register real history.

The preparation inventories exact BBO-1s catalog dates and TBBO catalog intervals
with mandatory exclusions, without opening values. Inventory intervals do not
prove daily quote coverage. Before any L1 values are accessed, freeze the final
strategy, forecast/trade ledger, permitted dates, schema and source identity.
Use exact same-contract snapshots at interval ends matching scheduled timestamps,
then one and five seconds later as separate sensitivities. Buy at ask references
and sell at bid references. Missing, invalid, crossed or observably stale quotes
remain unresolved; no favorable nearby search is permitted. Forward-filled
fields may have unknown freshness. An interval boundary is not application
receipt, and displayed size does not guarantee a fill.

TBBO comparisons remain explicitly trade-conditioned. Quote-side P&L subtracts
fees; additional-slippage sensitivity is separate. Historical spread allowances
are not subtracted again. Protected-date values consume information even in an
execution-only exercise. Any change informed by validation needs fresh
independent evidence; these diagnostics never establish alpha or deployment.
